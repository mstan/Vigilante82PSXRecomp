Vigilante 8: 2nd Offense @VERSION@

Extract the entire Windows ZIP, then run Vigilante82PSXRecomp.exe.

1. Select your complete Vigilante 8: 2nd Offense USA disc (SLUS-00868).
   CUE/BIN and CHD are supported. Netplay requires all 17 tracks.
   OpenBIOS is included; no retail BIOS file is needed.
2. Configure your gamepad or keyboard in the launcher, then press Play.
3. Optional original cars/music require your Vigilante 8 USA v1.0 disc.
   Super Dreamland 64 requires your Vigilante 8 USA N64 ROM.
   Select source media in Mods; preparation happens automatically.

Fresh installs use 4x internal resolution and native-wide Fit to Window.
Existing explicit mod/view choices are preserved when updating.

Both netplay players need the same release, complete discs and matching
content choices. Configure Player 1 / Netplay locally on each computer.
Choose 16:9 or 21:9 in Mods > Widescreen > View before starting, then open
Netplay to host/join a room or use LAN / Direct IP. Choose 2 Player in-game.
Each player gets an independent full-screen gameplay view.

High Frame Rate remains experimental. Playtest Diagnostics are optional and
off by default in this public release. If enabled, recordings under
saves/diagnostics contain starting memory-card data; share them deliberately.

Keep your saves and settings when updating. This package contains no discs,
donor ROMs, converted donor assets, retail BIOS images or saved games.
Read README.md and RELEASE_NOTES.md for features, setup and known limitations.
